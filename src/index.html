Our Craft

Twenty years at the cutting board

Chef Haruto Ito trained in Osaka before bringing a single, stubborn idea to the Midwest: rice first, fish second, everything else a distant third.

Rice is the dish

Our shari is seasoned with a three-year aged red vinegar and held at body temperature all night. It is the part of the meal nobody photographs and the part we argue about most.

One counter, one seating

Twelve seats, two services, no substitutions. The omakase changes daily depending on what clears the market in Chicago.
